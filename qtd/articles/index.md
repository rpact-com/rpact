- [Quantitative Trial Decisions for Means and Rates with
  rpact](https://docs.rpact.org/qtd/articles/rpact_quantitative_trial_design.md):

  This document illustrates frequentist and Bayesian quantitative Go,
  Indeterminate, and Stop decisions for continuous and binary endpoints
  with rpact. It covers priors, custom posterior criteria, both
  percentile interpretations, operating characteristics, summaries, QTD
  plots, interval methods, sensitivity analyses, and a complete
  numerical demonstration.
